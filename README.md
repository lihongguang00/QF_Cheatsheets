# NUS Quantitative Finance Undergraduate Modules Cheatsheets
Cheatsheets for undergraduate NUS Quantitative Finance modules. 

Notes/Word of Advices:
- All Cheatsheets are crafted by me, but some content might be left out due to space constraint, so please verify and use at your own risk.
- **DO NOT** use my cheatsheets blindly. The intention of uploading the cheatsheets is to help students with hectic schedules (multiple mods + internship) to accelerate the process of crafting their own cheatsheet. I do not condone taking shortcuts in learning, so please still take the time to do proper revision.
- If you require the docx file, please raise an issue in the repo with your NUS email address to reach out directly to me.
